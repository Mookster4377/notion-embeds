Adding videos

Open this page on your phone or computer, press ☰ Guide, and upload there. New videos show up on this TV within a few minutes, by themselves.

Upload to the channel

Saves videos or pictures to the channel, so every screen gets them, smart TVs included. They show up everywhere about a minute after uploading.

One-time setup on this device: uploads are saved in your GitHub repo , so this page needs a GitHub key.

1. Make a token: under Repository access pick Only select repositories → ; under Permissions set Contents to Read and write. Generate and copy it.

2. Paste it here. It stays on this device only and is sent only to GitHub.

Files up to 95 MB each, and about 1 GB for the whole site (GitHub's limits). For lots of videos or long ones, put them on YouTube (unlisted is fine) and paste the links below. Use ✕ in the lists below to take something off the channel.

Add YouTube videos in bulk: paste links, as many as you like (one per line, or straight from a list). They're added to the type picked above, with their YouTube titles.
