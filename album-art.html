Covers

Covers come from Apple Music, at 1000 × 1000.

Find covers

Looks up each song without a cover on Apple Music and adds the album cover, matching the artist and album (or the song title when there's no album). Songs that already have a cover are left alone.

Covers matched on the song title only are marked "check cover". Switch the list to Covers to check to go through them. Use "Find cover" on any song to pick a different one.

Backup

Everything you've done here lives inside this browser. Save it all into one backup file to keep it safe, move it to another browser or computer, or bring it back after clearing your browser.

Loading a backup adds what's in it to what's already here. A song that has artwork in both keeps the backup's. Nothing else is removed.

YouTube links

Bring in the links from YouTube Song Finder: its backup file (Save / load → Save backup file) or its CSV (Download list as CSV).

Only have an export?

Pick the Music Station export (the .zip, or the folder if you already unzipped it). Covers, renames and song files are put back on the right songs. A Plex Music export works too, matched by artist and title.
